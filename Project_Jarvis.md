# JARVIS — Project Foundation

## 1. Core Purpose

JARVIS is a **Personal AI Operating System** designed to provide a unified intelligence and execution layer for managing AI models, specialized agents, memory, tools, applications, and digital workflows.

Its purpose is to help its owner understand information, accomplish goals, automate tasks, and coordinate digital activities through a unified multimodal interface, while maintaining security, user control, and extensibility.

## 2. Primary Goals

JARVIS has three equally important goals:

1. **Automation:** Save time by automating repetitive tasks and workflows.
2. **Productivity:** Improve productivity by managing work, information, and digital activities.
3. **Intelligent Partnership:** Act as an intelligent partner that helps solve problems, make informed decisions, and achieve goals.

## 3. Target Users

JARVIS will follow a **personal-first, multi-user-ready** approach.

- **Initial focus:** A single primary user (the owner).
- **Future capability:** Support multiple users through separate identities, permissions, and isolated personal data.
- **Architectural principle:** Keep the system extensible for multi-user support without introducing unnecessary complexity into the initial development phase.

## 4. Operating Environments

JARVIS will target a broad, cross-platform operating environment.

It should eventually support:

- Desktop computers and laptops.
- Smartphones and tablets.
- Web browsers, websites, and web applications.
- Desktop applications and supported OS-level interactions.
- IoT devices, smart home systems, and other connected hardware.

**Implementation principle:** Support will be introduced incrementally, prioritizing the most valuable and practical integrations first.
